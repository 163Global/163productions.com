Onboard cameras

We rent cameras to the teams and install them. Their feeds go straight into the show, so the producer can cut to the cockpit at the moment that matters.

ON AIR → IN-CAR CUTS

Live tracking and timing

03 · Why us

We race.

We race these courses, and we run race registration and race websites. We see the details other crews never see.

  • Classes

    We know why a class starts where it does, and we don't put the wrong number on screen.

  • The dot

    When a GPS dot stops moving, we know whether that means a flat, a pit stop, or a dead zone, and we say so on air.

  • Race day

    We know where the course goes quiet, when the field spreads out, and where the story is at 2 AM.

  • Teams

    We know what a team needs from a broadcast: footage for their sponsors and proof their car was seen.

  • Promoters

    We know registration, tech and results from the inside, so the show fits how your event actually runs.
