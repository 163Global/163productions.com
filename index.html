The race production package

One crew brings the cameras, the tracking, the telemetry and the booth.

Everything below comes as one production. The cameras and telemetry are TRC Livestream Products, built by our own team: we rent them to racers and run them on race day.

TRC Livestream Products
TRC TERRACAST PRO

The in-car camera we rent at tech.

Installs in 60 seconds. Streams live video and reports the car's location the whole race. Your top racers all get an in-car feed, whether or not they own a camera.

TRC TERRACAST

The phone-based TerraCast.

Mounts in the cab or along the course. Streams live and reports its location, like the Pro.

TRC TELEMETRY

The engine, on screen.

Reads the car's engine computer: RPM, throttle, speed and temperatures. On Starlink it also sends the car's location.

SRT INGEST

Every common in-car stream.

Teams already streaming plug in with an SRT key, the broadcast standard. Their feed goes on air like ours.

AERIAL · TRACKSIDE

Drones, course cameras and interviews.

Over the sections where the leaders are close, at the pits and at the finish.

STREAMEASY

Spectators become cameras.

With our StreamEasy app, a phone at the side of the course is a feed the producer can pick.

LIVE DATA

Our own leaderboard, map and splits.

Built live from tracking, by class. Unofficial. Official results stay with the race.

Tracking

Race tracking drops out. So we bring our own.

We've watched official tracking go down mid-race too many times. Our map runs on three sources at once, so one can drop and viewers never see a blank map.

SOURCE 1

Every TRC TerraCast.

Each camera reports its car's live location.

SOURCE 2

Every TRC Telemetry box.

Sends location over Starlink, along with the engine data.

SOURCE 3

The official tracking company.

We take in their data too, and build our custom live data from all three.

Built by racers

Two racers built this show, and both are at your race.

We met on snowmobiles, deep in the Montana backcountry, a long way from any road. Between us we've been the racer, the co-driver, the mechanic in the pit at 2 a.m., and the parent watching a kid's dot. We build the broadcast we'd want our own families watching.

Co-founder

Andrew Priest

On a bike at 2, racing flat track at 4, motocross through the teens. Then drift cars built at home, stand-up jet skis and arenacross. Then UTVs, as the builder, the mechanic, the driver and the co-driver. Today it's desert racing on a bike and in a UTV.

Somewhere along the way it became a family thing. Ten years of prepping the kids' bikes, and now everyone races: jet skis, motos and UTVs. Some weekends the co-driver is Andrew's son.

Off the course, Andrew is an engineer and systems developer, and the operator in the businesses shared with Sean: a partner in TRC Racing Products and operations manager for Gearshift Garage Livestream Racing. Andrew also founded Deadset Solutions, which builds custom software for race registration, fabrication shop inventory and invoicing, and race car tracking, and owns Baja Sirens.

Race day: on the ground. TerraCasts into the cars, the connection out, racer data to the booth.

Co-founder

Sean Connell

A fabricator first. Sean ran an auto shop while still young, swapping motors and building Toyota-based buggies, and raced the desert on two wheels. Montana brought snowmobiles, built for freeriding deep in the backcountry.

Today Sean builds UTV race cars, races and co-drives UTVs for several teams, and is training to race Baja on a bike alongside Andrew.

Off the course, Sean is a software developer. Sean owns an IT company, co-founded TRC Racing Products, and built the DirtOS off-road navigation system. Everything our cameras run on is Sean's work, from the firmware inside the TRC TerraCast Pro to the livestreams and the tracking, along with much of the other tech behind the show.

Race day: at the desk. Feeds, graphics, data, and the announcers' mix.
