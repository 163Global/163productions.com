Why our coverage is different

Our announcers know who every racer is, where they are, and who they're chasing.

Most crews point cameras at a race. We also bring in your entry list, each racer's story from registration, and live position from tracking, all on one dashboard in the booth. So the call can sound like this:

How it works

What's behind the show.

The cameras, the tracking, the booth dashboard and the graphics. You don't have to run any of it, but here's how it works.

Cameras in the cars

Your racers rent the cameras. Nobody buys a box.

The hard part of in-car coverage is getting cameras into enough cars. We handle it: we bring a fleet of TRC TerraCast cameras, rent them to teams at tech, and put every feed into the show. Your teams don't have to buy anything.

TRC TERRACAST PRO

The in-car camera teams rent from us at tech.

Installs in 60 seconds. Streams live video and reports the car's location the whole race. Your top racers get an in-car feed, whether or not they own a camera.

ALREADY STREAMING?

Bring your own camera.

Any common in-car stream plugs into the show with an SRT key.

ENGINE DATA

TRC Telemetry puts RPM, throttle, speed and temperatures on screen for cars running it.

TerraCast cameras and TRC Telemetry are made by TRC. Teams that want to own one can buy from TRC. TRC products ↗

Tracking

Race tracking drops out. So we bring our own.

Official tracking can drop out mid-race. Our map pulls from three sources at once, so when one drops, the others can keep it going.

SOURCE 1

Every TRC TerraCast.

Each camera reports its car's live location.

SOURCE 2

Every TRC Telemetry box.

Sends location over Starlink, along with the engine data.

SOURCE 3

The official tracking company.

We take in their data too, and build our custom live data from all three.

Questions

What promoters ask us first.

What happens when official tracking goes down?
Our map can keep going on its other sources. Every rented TerraCast and every TRC Telemetry box reports its car's location, and we merge that with the official feed.
How is pricing determined?
Every production is quoted on its own, for a single race or a full series. Price depends on hours on air, the number of in-car and course cameras, course length and terrain, crew size and travel. We talk it through with you first, then send a written show plan and price.
Do our teams have to buy cameras?
No. We rent TerraCast cameras to teams at tech. Teams already streaming plug in with an SRT key. Teams that want to own one can buy from TRC.
Is your leaderboard official?
No. Our leaderboard, map and splits are built live from tracking so viewers can follow the race. Official results stay with you.
Whose brand is on the broadcast?
Yours. Graphics, leaderboards and racer cards are built in your colors and type. If you don't have a brand guide, we build the look for your broadcast.
How is this different from a race-tech platform?
A platform sells teams hardware and software, and your staff run it. We produce the finished show and run it on race day, with the data built into the broadcast.
Where does the show go?
Your YouTube channel, so the audience and the views stay with your race.
How long are you on air?
You set the show hours. Before race day you get the plan in writing: hours on air, which miles have cameras, and which are tracking only.
Do you cover a full series?
Yes. We produce single races and full series. Tell us what you're running and we'll plan it with you.
What do you need from us?
Your schedule, your entry list and your course. We take it from there, including collecting racer stories at registration.
