The dark miles

Most of a desert race happens where nobody can see it. Somewhere past mile 300, a mom is watching a GPS dot that hasn't moved in eleven minutes.

When that dot stops, we see it too, with the mile, the minute and the last engine reading. If we know why, we say so. If we don't, we say that.

The light in the dust

In the dust, you follow the amber light.

Desert trucks run an amber light at the rear. When the dust is too thick to see the hood in front of you, that light is how you find the truck ahead.

That's our job on race day. One clear picture through the noise, for the people who can't be out there.

DAY BEFORE

Cameras go in at tech.

We install onboard cameras during inspection. Teams rent them per race, so nobody buys a camera for one weekend. Spectators can add a feed from their phone with StreamEasy.

GPS

Every tracked car is on the map.

Positions update the course map and the leaderboard live. Names, numbers and classes come from registration, so the graphics match the entry list.

RPM · THR · °F

The engine reads out on screen.

Our telemetry box reads the engine computer: RPM, throttle, speed, temperatures. When a truck slows at RM 180, you can see whether it was the driver or the motor.

GAP 0:41 → 0:08

The gaps pick the shot.

Tracking measures the gap between cars all race. When one drops from 0:41 to 0:08, it gets flagged, and the producer cuts there before the pass.

SIGNAL LOST

We report it with the time.

When a dot stops or a feed drops, race control logs the mile and the minute. If we don't know why, we say we don't know.

SHOW HOURS

The promoter sets them.

Before race day you get the plan in writing: the hours we're on air, which miles have cameras, and which are tracking only.

MONDAY

The numbers go out.

Sponsors get their Seen Report: minutes on screen, every frame. Teams get their clips and their engine data.

Why us

We also build the registration systems some of these races run on. We know why a class starts where it does, and every number on screen is checked against the entry list.
