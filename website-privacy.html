Website privacy

Last updated 17 October 2026

This page covers 163productions.com and its contact form. Our StreamEasy phone app has its own policy: StreamEasy privacy policy.

What we collect

  • What you type into the contact form: your race organization, your name, your email address and, if you give it, your phone number.
  • When you sent it and which page you sent it from.

We don't use advertising trackers on this site, and we don't collect anything from visitors who don't fill in the form.

Why

Only to reply to you about producing your race or series. We don't sell or rent your details, we don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't send you text messages.

Where it goes

  • Your message is emailed to the two founders of 163 Productions, and a copy is saved in a private spreadsheet so no inquiry gets lost. Both are kept in our Google account.
  • We send you one email confirming we got your message.

How long we keep it

As long as we're talking with you about a production, and after that only for our records of who contacted us. Ask and we'll delete it.

Your choices

  • To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email us at support@163productions.com.
